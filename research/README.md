## Journal Publication

### Implementation of the Discrete Area Method and Its Impact on the Steel Reinforcement of Large Mat Foundations

**Authors:** Hassan Alzoaby, Gaby Saad, and Grace Abou-Jaoude  
**Journal:** Innovative Infrastructure Solutions  
**Published:** 08 March 2025  
**Volume / Article:** 10, 131  
**DOI:** 10.1007/s41062-025-01926-x

This paper investigates the discrete area method for large mat foundations and its effect on reinforcement design when variable modulus of subgrade reaction values are used instead of a single constant value.

[View journal article on Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41062-025-01926-x)
