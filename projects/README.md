## Featured Project

### DoD ESTCP PFAS Source-Zone Remediation Demonstration

**Project:** Comprehensive Assessment of Applying Modified Clays Using Jet Grouting for In Situ Isolation of PFAS Source Zones  
**Program:** U.S. Department of Defense — Environmental Security Technology Certification Program (ESTCP)  
**PhD Advisor / Principal Academic Mentor:** Dr. Chadi El Mohtar, The University of Texas at Austin

This field-scale Department of Defense project evaluated the use of modified clay with jet grouting and conventional soil mixing to stabilize PFAS source zones and reduce continued migration to groundwater. The demonstration compared treatment approaches, assessed the integrity of isolation elements, evaluated PFAS stability in treatment spoils, and supported development of a practical remediation strategy for sites with deep groundwater and PFAS-impacted unsaturated soils.

#### My Role

I served as the **lead graduate student on the project throughout my PhD**, working under the supervision of my PhD advisor, **Dr. Chadi El Mohtar**.

My responsibilities extended across both the technical and project-management sides of the work. I helped manage and coordinate day-to-day project activities and served as a central technical point of contact across the university research team, consultants, contractors, and project stakeholders.

My work included:

- Leading and performing laboratory testing related to PFAS treatment, soil behavior, and material performance
- Developing and running numerical and engineering models to support interpretation and project decisions
- Processing, analyzing, and interpreting laboratory and field data
- Coordinating technical work with consultants and specialty contractors
- Supporting planning and execution of field activities
- Participating in project meetings with clients, contractors, consultants, and other stakeholders
- Preparing and delivering technical presentations and project updates
- Communicating results, limitations, and recommended next steps to technical and non-technical audiences
- Supporting technical reports, research deliverables, and documentation
- Managing multiple workstreams while maintaining continuity between laboratory research, modeling, field implementation, and project objectives

The project gave me direct experience working at the interface of **research, consulting, field construction, environmental remediation, numerical modeling, laboratory experimentation, and client communication**. It also required translating detailed research findings into decisions that could be used by contractors, consultants, and DoD stakeholders during a real field demonstration.
