Welcome to my engineering and research portfolio. This repository highlights selected academic research, engineering projects, technical presentations, publications, awards, and computational tools developed throughout my graduate research and professional engineering work.

## About Me

I am a geo-environmental and geotechnical engineer with experience spanning subsurface characterization, foundation and settlement analysis, numerical modeling, environmental remediation, engineering data analysis, and Python-based automation.

My work combines engineering judgment with computational tools to solve complex geotechnical and environmental problems, communicate results clearly, and improve technical workflows.
